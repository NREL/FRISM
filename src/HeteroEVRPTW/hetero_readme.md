As 100% truck electrification have not been achieved, the freight transport companies may have fleet combinations. for example a company may want to use portion of ICEV and EV for their trucking routing and delivery solution.  
We considered heterogeenous fleet combination for our ETRPTW problem to represent real world case. The fleet combination are: ICEV which does not have fuel constraints and EV: which has battery constraints and inclusion of on-route charging operations. We also considered different customer service time factors and different fixed vehicle costs to give somewhat importance to EV use.  
First of all, we will solve our routing solution using heterogeneous fleet routing using Google OR tools. We obtain fleet combination there. As ICEV doesnot have routing fuel or other constraints, we assumed the routes are final who are served through ICEVs. Only those who are said to be served by EV is further processed for electric routing with charging station inclusion and optimality through VNS algorithm implementation.  
The final routes have been combined at last with ICEV routes plus final optimized EV routes after algorithm processing.
